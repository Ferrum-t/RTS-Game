## Mobility & logistics (later)

- Horse-based transport capacity for migration
- Settlement mass / weight → affects pack time, move speed, unpack time
- Settlement density / inertia: more buildings → stronger but harder to move
- Migration signals visible to enemies ("neighbouring aul is packing")
- Temporary traces left after leaving a site (camp remains, loot, points of interest)

## Economy & animals

- Horses as simultaneous: resource + cavalry limit + migration capacity
- Capture of animals from enemy stables
- Seasonal / migrating herds as map pressure

## Environment

- Dynamic climate / fertility zones that force migration
- Day/night, sandstorm, weather affecting combat and movement

## Society & identity

- Faction-specific mobility styles (speed vs toughness vs pack time)
- Tier progression that specifically reduces mobility *penalties*
- Abandoned buildings vs fully mobile aul

## UI / feel

- Clear player feedback when a building is in reduced-efficiency state
- Visual transformation of models between DEPLOYED and MOBILE
